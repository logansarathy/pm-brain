# Key Frameworks

This section lists simple frameworks and tools useful for the Foundation module. Use them to structure your thinking and work.

- **Double Diamond:** A design framework with two main phases: Discover & Define (understand the problem) followed by Develop & Deliver (create and launch solutions).
- **Empathy Map:** A visualization of a user’s *says, thinks, does,* and *feels* to synthesize user research insights.
- **Customer/ Journey Mapping:** A visual timeline of a user’s experience when achieving a goal. Helps see pain points and opportunities.
- **SMART Goals:** A way to set effective goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).
- **Impact vs Effort Matrix:** A prioritization grid to balance quick wins (high impact, low effort) against bigger bets.
- **Feedback Loop (PDCA):** Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle to iterate on products or learning tasks continuously.

Each framework is a tool to help you think like a product manager: empathize with users, define clear goals, and iterate rapidly.
