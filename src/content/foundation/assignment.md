# Assignment: My PM Journey

## Objective

Reflect on your motivations and goals. This assignment helps you articulate *why* you are doing this fellowship and what you aim to achieve. This becomes your first portfolio artifact.

## Scenario

You have just begun a 16-week product management fellowship. The mentors want to understand your background and aspirations so they can tailor guidance.

## Tasks

1. Write a short introduction about yourself (background, interests).
2. Explain *why* you want to become a product manager.
3. Describe your career goals and what success looks like for you.
4. Identify one major challenge you might face in this journey and how you plan to overcome it.

## Deliverables

A concise write-up (1–2 pages) titled **“My Product Management Journey”**, covering the points above.

## Submission Format

Markdown or PDF document.

## Evaluation Rubric

Your work will be evaluated on:
- **Clarity of Goals:** How clearly you express your career objectives.
- **Reflection Depth:** Thoughtfulness about your motivations and challenges.
- **Presentation:** Quality of writing (organized, well-written) and formatting.
- **Actionability:** Specific steps or habits mentioned to achieve your goals.

Remember, this is the start of your portfolio; be honest and reflective.
