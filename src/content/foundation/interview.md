# Interview Prep

To prepare for product management interviews, practice both behavioral and product sense questions.

## Behavioral Questions

- **Tell me about yourself and why you want to be a PM.** (Your elevator pitch for product management.)
- **Describe a time you worked in a team to solve a problem.** (Focus on your role and impact.)
- **How do you prioritize tasks when everything feels important?** (Shows organization and decision-making.)
- **Give an example of a mistake you made and how you handled it.** (Reflects learning and accountability.)
- **Where do you see yourself in 5 years?** (Career goals and commitment.)

## Product-Sense Questions

- **Design a new feature for [common product, e.g. YouTube]. How would you decide what to build?** 
- **How would you improve a low-rated app (e.g. airline booking app)?** 
- **Pick a product you use every day. Describe its strengths and weaknesses.** 
- **How would you launch a new product in India? What factors would you consider?**

## Sample Answers (Tips)

- **Tell me about yourself:** Briefly highlight relevant skills or experiences (projects, tech skills, or past roles) and your passion for solving customer problems.
- **Design question:** Define the problem first. Ask clarifying questions (as in *The Mom Test*, focus on user needs, not assumptions). Propose a simple MVP solution.
- **Improvement question:** Talk about gathering user feedback, analyzing data, and using frameworks (impact vs effort) to prioritize changes.

## General Tips

- Talk in terms of **users and data**. For product questions, don’t just list features—explain the *why*.
- Use the **STAR** method for behavioral: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
- Keep answers concise and structured. Use examples from real life when possible.
