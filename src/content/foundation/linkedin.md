# LinkedIn Post

## Draft

Today I started **Week 0** of my 16-week Product Management fellowship with PM OS! 🎉 I'm excited to dive into product thinking and build real skills. 

## Hook

- "After months of research, I'm finally on the journey to become a product manager."
- "Day 1 of my 16-week PM OS fellowship—here's what I'm looking forward to."

## Story

Talk about your motivation: maybe you built something in college, or solved a problem for a friend, and now want to solve bigger problems. Example: “Growing up, I was always curious about how products I use every day were created. I studied engineering, but now I want to learn *end-to-end product creation*. Starting this fellowship is a big step toward that goal.”

## CTA (Call to Action)

- Ask a question to engage: “Fellow product enthusiasts, what’s one product improvement you wish you could make?”
- Invite comments: “Share your tips for starting a learning journey!”
