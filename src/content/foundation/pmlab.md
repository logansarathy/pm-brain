# PM Lab: Observing User Problems

## Goal

Develop a PM’s habit of noticing real user problems in everyday life.

## Challenge

Identify **5 user problems** around you today that you think are worth solving.

## Experiment

Go on a 30–45 minute observation stroll (around your home, school, or neighborhood). Be curious: look at people using products or services. Pay attention to any frustrations or needs. Write down anything that seems inefficient, confusing, or difficult for a user.

(Field studies and observing users in their natural environment are powerful research methods.)

## Deliverables

Submit a list of **5 problems**, each with:
- A brief description of the problem.
- Who is affected (who is the user).
- Why it matters (what frustration it causes).

Optionally, include photos or sketches if safe and appropriate.

## Reflection

- Which of the identified problems seems most important or urgent? Why?
- How might a product manager approach solving that problem? (Think about talking to users or prototyping a solution.)

By doing this, you practice user-centric thinking. Even simple observation can uncover valuable insights for product ideas.
