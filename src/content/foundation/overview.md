# Overview
# Foundation: Getting Started with Product Management

The Foundation module is an introductory mini-bootcamp (1–2 days) that prepares you for the 16-week Product Management OS program. You will learn the program structure, how to learn effectively, and the basics of what product management means. By the end of Foundation, you should understand the program flow (lessons → quiz → assignment → PM Lab), and have created your first deliverable for your portfolio.

Foundation covers:
- Orientation to PM OS and community.
- What product management is (guiding a product through its lifecycle).
- Learning approaches: building, user research, and reflection.
- Creating your first portfolio artifact (writing your PM journey).

Earn 200 XP in this module. Complete 3 lessons, 1 quiz, 1 assignment, and 1 PM Lab. Let’s start building your product mindset!
