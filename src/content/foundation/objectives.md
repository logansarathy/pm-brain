## Learning Objectives
The Foundation module prepares you to begin the fellowship successfully. By the end of Foundation, you will be able to:

- **Define Product Management:** Explain what a Product Manager does and how PMs work (the core role in guiding products).
- **Understand PM OS Structure:** Describe the 16-week curriculum flow and learning process (lesson → quiz → assignment → PM Lab).
- **Set Up for Success:** Prepare your workspace, tools, and weekly schedule; set a personal learning goal for Week 1.
- **Apply Early Research Skills:** Conduct simple user observations and interviews (as emphasized by *The Mom Test*).
- **Create Your First Portfolio Artifact:** Reflect on your product journey and career goals by writing your “My PM Journey” assignment.
