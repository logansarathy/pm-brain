# Resources

**Articles & Blogs:**
- [The Nielsen Norman Group – Contextual Inquiry](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/contextual-inquiry/) – How observing users in context uncovers hidden insights.  
- [The Nielsen Norman Group – Journey Mapping 101](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/journey-mapping-101/) – Basics of mapping the user experience.  
- [ProductPlan – Ultimate Guide to Product Management](https://www.productplan.com/learn/product-management/) – Comprehensive overview of PM roles and processes.  
- [Mind the Product – Product Management Articles](https://www.mindtheproduct.com/what-is-product-management/) – Expert community articles on PM fundamentals.

**Books:**
- *Inspired* by Marty Cagan – A leading book on building products customers love (foundational PM principles).  
- *The Mom Test* by Rob Fitzpatrick – Guide on talking to customers to learn real needs (user interviews).  
- *Lean Startup* by Eric Ries – On validated learning and iterative product development.

**Videos & Podcasts:**
- [Product School YouTube Channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/ProductSchool) – Short videos on PM topics and career tips.  
- [The Product Podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-product-podcast/id1639711946) – Interviews with top product leaders.
