# Lesson 1: Welcome to PM OS

## Learning Outcomes

After this lesson, you will:
- Understand the goals and structure of this fellowship.
- Know how lessons, quizzes, and assignments work together.
- Learn how to track your progress (XP, streaks, portfolio).
- Feel motivated and ready to begin learning.

---

## Why This Matters

Today marks the start of your Product Management journey. Just like any product, the program has a roadmap. Understanding **what to expect** and **how to navigate** the course is crucial. We will also introduce the mindset of continuous learning and improvement – a key trait of successful product managers.

---

## Theory

**Product Management OS (PM OS)** is a learning platform that simulates real PM work. You progress by completing lessons (theory), passing quizzes (knowledge checks), doing assignments (projects for your portfolio), and practicing PM Labs (hands-on experiments). Each week builds on the previous, culminating in a capstone. This course is **project-based** – you learn by doing. In fact, product expert Marty Cagan notes that effective PMs ensure products meet user needs and business goals; we practice this mindset from day 1.

**Key Points:**
- **Program Flow:** Lesson → Quiz → Assignment → PM Lab → Reflection.
- **Progress:** Earn XP for each task. Track your streaks and hours.
- **Community:** Engage in discussions (future feature) and share your portfolio.

## Example

Imagine you’re learning to play guitar. You wouldn’t just read a book; you’d pick up a guitar and try songs. Similarly, PM OS has you apply concepts in short projects. For example, to learn user interviews, you’ll actually talk to a potential user.
