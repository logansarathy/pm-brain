# Practice Exercises

1. **Product I Love:** Name one product you use daily (app, tool, etc.). Describe *why* it works well for you.
2. **Product I Find Frustrating:** Name one product you use that often annoys or frustrates you. Describe the pain point.
3. **Improvement Idea:** Pick one everyday product (could be physical or digital) and think of one simple improvement or feature you would add.
4. **User Problem Noticed:** Walk around your home or neighborhood for 10 minutes. Note one problem or inconvenience you see others experiencing (e.g., long lines, confusing signage, etc.).

## Reflection

- What common themes did you notice across the products you chose?
- How did it feel to analyze products this way?
- How can these observations help you generate ideas for future product improvements?
